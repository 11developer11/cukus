How to Play

🕹️ Move: drag / swipe anywhere (or WASD / arrow keys).
🏹 Your hero auto-fires at the nearest enemy — just stay alive and keep moving!
💰 Kill enemies for coins (and XP) — this is a tycoon at heart: fight to earn, then spend to grow.
🧱 Around your Keep you'll see floating build signs in two rings — tap one to build a Wall (🪙50, permanent and indestructible, physically blocks enemies) or a Tower: 🪖 Soldier Post (balanced), 💣 Cannon Tower (heavy splash damage), or 🔮 Mage Spire (pierces multiple enemies). Towers can't be destroyed either — tap a built tower's sign again to upgrade it, endlessly, each level noticeably stronger than the last. Defenses are rebuilt fresh each run.
⭐ Level up mid-run to pick powerful upgrades.
👹 A BOSS appears every 10 waves, each one exponentially tougher than the last — and every wave in between gets harder too. Survive 70 waves to reach GOD LEVEL.
🏰 Gold earned is banked to the Kingdom Treasury. Spend it in the Shop on Hero upgrades and your Garrison (permanent tower damage & fire-rate bonuses) — these have no level cap and double in strength every single purchase, forever. Also spend it on your Keep, which grows through four material tiers, Wood → Steel → Titanium → Futuristic, each stronger tier also passively generating a trickle of coins on its own while you fight, tycoon-style.
🛡 A fully-built Futuristic Keep also grants an Energy Shield that blocks hits and slowly recharges.
🔁 After reaching God Level you can Ascend for a harder, more rewarding new journey.
