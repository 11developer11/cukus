How to Play

🕹️ Move: drag / swipe anywhere (or WASD / arrow keys).
🏹 Your hero auto-fires at the nearest enemy — just stay alive and keep moving!
💰 Walk over gold and XP orbs to collect them.
⭐ Level up mid-run to pick powerful upgrades.
👹 A BOSS appears every 10 waves. Survive 70 waves to reach GOD LEVEL.
🛕 Gold earned is banked to the Kingdom Treasury — spend it on permanent upgrades between runs.
🔁 After reaching God Level you can Ascend for a harder, more rewarding new journey.
